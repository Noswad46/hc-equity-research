## Not covered, and why

Two of the largest vaccine businesses in the world — **GSK** and **Sanofi** — are
absent from this page, along with **AstraZeneca** and **BioNTech**. On a page
about vaccines that is a conspicuous hole, so it should not be left to be
noticed.

All four report under IFRS. Under IAS 38 development costs that qualify are
capitalised and amortised, whereas US GAAP expenses essentially all R&D as
incurred. R&D intensity and cash runway, two of the headline measures here, are
computed straight off those lines, so the same spending produces materially
different figures depending only on which standard the company reports under.
Placing them in the same ranked table would imply a comparison that the
underlying accounting does not support. Several of them also file annually with
the SEC rather than quarterly, so the discrete quarterly series this site is
built on does not exist for them.

They are excluded rather than approximated. Putting a number in a table is a
claim that it means the same thing as the numbers beside it, and here it would
not.

**Read this page accordingly.** It covers the US GAAP-reporting part of the
vaccines and infectious disease universe. It is not a ranking of the global
vaccines industry, and a company's absence says nothing about its size,
prospects, or pipeline. See [the methodology
note](/methodology#coverage-exclusions) for the full reasoning.
